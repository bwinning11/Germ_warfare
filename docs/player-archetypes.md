# Player Archetypes & Review Lenses

Audience segments for *Germ Warfare* — and the lenses we use to pressure-test design decisions. The game is a genre **fusion** (strategy × deckbuilder × sim/roguelite), so the sweet-spot player loves *two or more* of these. We can't fully satisfy every purist; these profiles say who we're courting, who we're stretching, and where each is at risk of bouncing.

Use them two ways: (1) as **target audience** when prioritizing features, and (2) as **review lenses** — read the design as each fan and ask "does this land for them?"

**Priority key:** ⭐ Primary (design *for* them) · ◐ Secondary (court, don't center) · ○ Stretch (don't lock out).

---

## ⭐ The Strategy Super Fan
*Total War, Civ, StarCraft, Paradox.*
- **Wants:** depth, meaningful decisions, a long mastery curve, asymmetry, systems that reward thinking. Allergic to dumbing-down and RNG-over-skill.
- **Delights here:** the dive's tactical depth (active-pause), geoscape triage (expand / exploit / defend), the dual-win strategic identity, asymmetric classes, the fractal greed-vs-escape.
- **Risk of losing them:** the lean "selector + react" geoscape could read as *shallow* — they may crave the deeper strategist/grand-campaign layer we deferred. Watch macro depth; lean on the Game Speed → playstyle tilt to add strategic texture.

## ⭐ The Deckbuilder Fan
*Slay the Spire, Monster Train, Balatro.*
- **Wants:** tight synergies, build variety, draft decisions, the "one more run" loop, satisfying card/relic combos.
- **Delights here:** cards + relics (relics = your evolutions), horizontal unlocks, run variety, Escape vs. Burst as deck-archetypes.
- **Risk:** we deliberately *shrank* the active deck (a light reactive hand) — they may want more in-the-moment deckbuilding. Evolution-as-relics must *feel* like a build, not a stat trickle; synergies need real depth.

## ◐ The Sim / Plague Inc Fan
*Plague Inc, Pandemic, management sims.*
- **Wants:** the "watch the world fall" god-fantasy, systemic emergence, satisfying spread, the dispassionate-overseer feeling.
- **Delights here:** the geoscape, herd immunity / SIR, auto-spread, the clinical-sinister tone, the planet turning your color — the spine speaks straight to them.
- **Risk:** the **dive** (real-time tactics) pulls focus from the macro sim they came for; purists may resent being pulled into battles. Mitigate: dives are *chosen* ("pick your battles"), not constant; auto-spread carries the macro.

## ◐ The RTS Fan
*StarCraft, They Are Billions, C&C.*
- **Wants:** real-time tactical thrill, base / economy / army management, cascade-pressure tension.
- **Delights here:** the dive — active-pause, strains / colonies, the biomass economy, the TAB-style cascade Heat; and the eventual deeper RTS.
- **Risk:** "not enough RTS" — directed-spread is deliberately *not* unit micro; APM-lovers may find it light or slow. We chose this on purpose; court them with depth and feel, not twitch.

## ◐ The Roguelike Fan
*Hades, Dead Cells, FTL.*
- **Wants:** run variety, meaningful reset, build diversity, fair-but-punishing mastery.
- **Delights here:** the run structure, regional / host / zoonotic variety, horizontal unlocks, the dual-win replay, achievement-gated mastery.
- **Risk:** samey runs if the variety levers underdeliver; some expect *vertical* meta-growth (Hades' Mirror) we deliberately cut — frame the carrot as variety + mastery, not power.

## ○ The Curious Newcomer
*Drawn by the theme and tone, not genre-hardcore.*
- **Wants:** approachability, clear onboarding, a vivid fantasy, not being overwhelmed.
- **Delights here:** the "be a disease, take the world" hook, the clinical-sinister style, a chill long Game Speed.
- **Risk:** two scales + the counter-intuitive "survive, don't kill" can overwhelm. Onboarding (an open thread) decides whether they stay. Don't design *for* them — but don't wall them out.

---

## How to use these in review

When pressure-testing the design (or a specific decision), walk it past each ⭐ and ◐ lens:
- **Strategy super fan** — is there enough depth and meaningful decision-making?
- **Deckbuilder fan** — do builds have real synergy and variety?
- **Sim fan** — does the world-falling fantasy stay front and center?
- **RTS fan** — does the dive feel good to play moment-to-moment?
- **Roguelike fan** — is there enough run-to-run variety and mastery?

A design that delights both ⭐ primaries (strategy + deckbuilder) while keeping the ◐ secondaries courted is on target.
