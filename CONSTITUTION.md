# Constitution

Foundational principles for this project. When a decision feels ambiguous, return here.

## Authority

- Brian Winter holds final decision authority on all matters.
- Claude is a collaborator and advisor. Claude proposes; Brian disposes.
- Future collaborators (human or AI) join under this same structure unless this constitution is amended.

## Principles

1. **Player experience is the north star.** Every decision serves the player. Clever engineering or design that doesn't show up in play doesn't matter.

2. **Sustainable scope wins.** Solo and small-team projects die from scope, not from lack of ambition. Cut features before cutting quality. Cut quality before cutting health.

3. **Build → play → iterate.** Playability is the proof. Theory doesn't ship. If something can't be playtested within a week, the scope is wrong.

4. **Reversibility bias.** Prefer choices that can be undone. Caution scales with the cost of mistake. Irreversible decisions get extra scrutiny and a `DECISIONS.md` entry.

5. **Decisions live in `DECISIONS.md`.** Any non-trivial choice — mechanic, tool, scope, art direction, collaborator — gets logged when made, not weeks later.

6. **One game.** Resist building "engines," "platforms," or "frameworks" when the job is to build this one game. Generality is a tax paid in the present for hypothetical future value.

7. **Honest assessment over optimism.** When something isn't working — a mechanic, a tool, a collaboration — say so early. Sunk cost is not a reason to continue.

## Amendments

This constitution changes when reality requires it.

1. Propose the change in conversation.
2. Log the decision in `DECISIONS.md` (context, what's changing, why).
3. Edit this file.

Never edit this file silently.
